# Financial Engineering Model

This document outlines the design of a sophisticated financial engineering model for pricing and managing the debt tokens.

## Model Components

The financial engineering model will consist of the following components:

*   **A pricing model:** This will be used to determine the initial price of the debt tokens. The pricing model will take into account a variety of factors, such as the underlying toll revenue, the maturity date of the tokens, and the current interest rate environment.
*   **A risk management model:** This will be used to identify and mitigate the risks associated with the debt tokens. The risk management model will take into account a variety of factors, such as the volatility of the underlying toll revenue, the creditworthiness of the tolling authority, and the liquidity of the tokens.
*   **A portfolio management model:** This will be used to manage the portfolio of debt tokens. The portfolio management model will take into account a variety of factors, such as the investor's risk tolerance, the desired return on investment, and the overall market conditions.

## Model Implementation

The financial engineering model will be implemented using a combination of a front-end interface and a back-end engine. The front-end interface will be used by investors to view and manage their portfolios. The back-end engine will be used to perform the complex calculations required by the model.

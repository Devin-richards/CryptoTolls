# National Tolling Architecture

This document outlines the design of a national tolling architecture that can be adopted by multiple tolling authorities across the country.

## Guiding Principles

*   **Interoperability:** The system should be able to work with existing tolling systems and standards.
*   **Scalability:** The system should be able to handle a large volume of transactions.
*   **Security:** The system should be secure and protect the privacy of users.
*   **Flexibility:** The system should be flexible enough to accommodate the different needs of different tolling authorities.

## System Components

The national tolling architecture will consist of the following components:

*   **A central registry:** This will be a central database of all tolling authorities, toll roads, and users.
*   **A payment gateway:** This will process payments from users and distribute them to the appropriate tolling authorities.
*   **A data analytics platform:** This will collect and analyze data on traffic patterns and toll revenue.
*   **A user-facing application:** This will allow users to manage their accounts, pay their tolls, and view their transaction history.

## Implementation Plan

The implementation of the national tolling architecture will be divided into the following phases:

1.  **Phase 1: Pilot program:** We will launch a pilot program with a small number of tolling authorities to test the system and gather feedback.
2.  **Phase 2: National rollout:** We will roll out the system to all tolling authorities across the country.
3.  **Phase 3: International expansion:** We will expand the system to other countries.

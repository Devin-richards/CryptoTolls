Commitment to Compliance & Regulatory Standards

Crypto Tolls is dedicated to operating at the highest levels of regulatory compliance and ethical standards. We understand the critical importance of adhering to legal frameworks for tolling, financial transactions, and data security. This page outlines our approach to key compliance areas.

Tolling Regulations & Standards

Navigating the complex landscape of tolling regulations is paramount for providing a reliable and lawful service. Our platform is designed with these requirements at its core.

MAP-21 Compliance

Crypto Tolls is designed to align with the relevant provisions of the Moving Ahead for Progress in the 21st Century Act (MAP-21), facilitating streamlined toll collection and management for interoperability and efficiency in federally-funded highway projects. We support electronic toll collection standards and data security measures as outlined by MAP-21 guidelines.

State & Federal Tolling Laws

We proactively monitor and adapt to various state and federal tolling laws to ensure our services meet all necessary legal requirements across different jurisdictions. Our system architecture allows for flexibility in implementing region-specific rules and rate structures.

Commercial Fleet Operations

For commercial fleet operators, maintaining compliance is crucial. Crypto Tolls offers features that help fleets manage toll expenses, access detailed transaction records, and simplify reporting in accordance with transportation regulations.

Financial Integrity: KYC/AML Procedures

To ensure the security of financial transactions and prevent illicit activities, particularly with the integration of tokenized assets and cryptocurrency payments, Crypto Tolls implements robust Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) procedures.

Know Your Customer (KYC)

Our KYC process involves verifying the identity of our users to a reasonable standard, helping to prevent fraud and ensure that individuals are who they claim to be. This is a foundational step in securing our platform and complying with financial regulations.

Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

We employ AML measures to detect and deter money laundering activities. This includes transaction monitoring and reporting suspicious activities as required by law, contributing to a safer financial ecosystem for all users.

Data Privacy & Security in Compliance

While undertaking KYC/AML procedures, we remain steadfast in our commitment to user data privacy and security. All personal information is handled in accordance with strict data protection regulations, ensuring that compliance does not come at the expense of user trust.

Audit Support & System Transparency

Crypto Tolls is built with transparency and auditability in mind. Our data systems are designed to generate comprehensive records and reports that can support internal reviews and federal or state audits. We are committed to providing clear, verifiable information to meet regulatory oversight requirements.

For more detailed information on our compliance programs, please contact our support team.

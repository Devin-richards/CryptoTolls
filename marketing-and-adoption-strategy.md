# Marketing and Adoption Strategy

This document outlines the marketing and adoption strategy for the new crypto tolls platform.

## Target Audience

Our target audience is twofold:

*   **Tolling authorities:** We will target tolling authorities of all sizes, from small, independent operators to large, state-run agencies.
*   **Drivers:** We will target drivers who are looking for a more convenient and cost-effective way to pay their tolls.

## Marketing Channels

We will use a variety of marketing channels to reach our target audience, including:

*   **Industry publications:** We will advertise in industry publications that are read by tolling authority officials.
*   **Trade shows:** We will exhibit at trade shows that are attended by tolling authority officials.
*   **Social media:** We will use social media to reach drivers and promote the benefits of the new platform.
*   **Public relations:** We will work with the media to generate positive press coverage of the new platform.

## Adoption Strategy

Our adoption strategy is to start with a small number of pilot programs and then gradually expand to a national rollout. We will work closely with the pilot program participants to ensure that the platform meets their needs and that they are satisfied with the results. Once we have a successful track record, we will begin to market the platform to a wider audience.
